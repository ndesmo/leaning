Summary:
1. L^N_γ ≤ Lcl(γ) + L1(γ) for γ ≥ 3/2.
2. Odd star graphs with N ≥ 3: L_{γ,N} ≤ Lcl + L1/N.
3. The conjectured bound 2L1(γ) holds below the critical envelope 6a⁻² sech²(x/a).
4. Necessary conditions on a counterexample.

The conjecture L^N_γ = 2L1(γ) on (3/2, 5/2] remains open.
